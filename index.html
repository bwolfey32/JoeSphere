The Rogansphere Unofficial fan project
About this project & how the numbers are made

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Joe Rogan. The Rogansphere tracks announced public appearances — ticketed dates, scheduled broadcasts, and episode releases — and hosts Posts, a public feed of clips and reactions shared by other fans. It is not a live location feed and never will be.

Entries marked Unconfirmed are real scheduled events where his attendance has not been verified. Entries badged Demo are generated placeholders, not real dates.

Comments, posts and reactions are all public — visible to every visitor, attributed to whatever display name the poster typed in, and not reviewed before they appear. Nothing you write here is private.

Studio locations are an estimate. No public source places individual episodes, so the recording base shown on the map and in Insights is derived from each episode's release date — which is not its recording date, and some episodes were recorded remotely. Event venues, by contrast, are sourced.

Money figures separate reported gate and attendance from a clearly-marked estimated band for YouTube advertising only. The estimate is a range, never a headline number.

Maintenance tools

These write to this browser only — nothing here is published or shared.

Import a list

One per line: date | what it is | city | type. Type is optional (standup, broadcast, podcast). Or just paste raw lines — anything with a readable date ("Aug. 15, 2026", "8/15/2026", "2026-08-15") gets picked up, and a known city is matched out of the text.

Log an appearance
